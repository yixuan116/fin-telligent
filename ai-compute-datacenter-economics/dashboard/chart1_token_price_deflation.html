Token Price Deflation: The Race to Zero

Blended price per 1M tokens (75% input / 25% output weighting) for ten flagship models, plotted against the P25–P75 range across all 520 tracked models, 44,775 pricing observations, weekly from 2024-06-03 to 2026-07-20.

Each flagship model is tracked at a fixed list price for its lifetime in this dataset — deflation shows up not as individual price cuts, but as the industry P25–P75 band sliding down as cheaper new entrants launch. Click a legend entry to isolate or hide a series.
