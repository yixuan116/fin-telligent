💲 Price Tag: what models cost and how 1,000 requests get billed

            
Three levers set the bill: which tier you pick, whether you cache, whether you batch.

            
Prices: LLM Price-Performance Tracker: (March 2026) · live API rates collected March 31, 2026


            
Tiers are cut on blended price (3 input : 1 output). Each tier starts roughly 6 to 10x above the last.

Triggers when the prompt starts with content you sent recently

                  
Why cheaper: repeated content needs no recompute

Triggers when you submit jobs in bulk and wait up to 24h

                  
Why cheaper: waiting lets providers fill idle GPU time
