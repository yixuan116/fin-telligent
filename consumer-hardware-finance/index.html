Consumer Hardware Finance: Build to Sell

A production ramp drives one shared volume number through build economics, cash, and the sell-side P&L

Assumptions

  • 1 contract manufacturer (CM) plant — outsourced assembly, no owned factory
  • 2 product lines: Product A (Premium), Product B (Standard)
  • BOM includes both imported core and locally sourced peripheral components
  • Business model: hardware unit sales + recurring software/AI subscription
  • 2 markets: Domestic (US) + Overseas
  • 2 channels: DTC (online, direct) + Omnichannel (offline, retail/distributor)
  • All logistics via 3PL — no owned logistics infrastructure
  • Pure B2C consumer end-users
  • Unified MSRP across channels; distributors operate on wholesale price after channel margin deduction
  • All figures are simulated for framework demonstration purposes
